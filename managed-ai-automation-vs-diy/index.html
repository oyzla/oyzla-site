Questions about managed AI automation

What is managed AI automation?

A service where the provider designs, builds, hosts and runs the AI tools for your business. At Oyzla AI the AI employees draft the repetitive work (invoices, customer replies, quotes, scheduling, document and data entry) and you approve it from one dashboard.

How is managed AI automation different from DIY AI software?

With DIY software you configure, connect and maintain the tools yourself. With a managed service the provider does that work and keeps it running. Oyzla AI is a managed service, not software you set up by yourself.

Who runs the system after it goes live?

We do. We host and run it on our own accounts and hosting, and the monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring and improvements, so you manage nothing.

What do I own?

You own the design, the configuration and the IP. The live system runs on our keys and hosting as a managed service and ends with the contract. Your own data and accounts were always yours.

Do you hold my passwords?

No. You connect your own accounts through OAuth, and Oyzla AI never holds your passwords.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel anytime. Billing stops at the end of the current paid period and the service stops with it. On request we export the data we hold for you. The refund and cancellation policy spells out exactly what stops.

Does the AI act without my approval?

No. Everything is approval-gated by default, and nothing goes out under your name until you approve it.
