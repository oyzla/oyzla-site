Refund Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026 · Questions? Message us through the chat or email info@oyzlaleads.com.

The Short Version

Our guarantee: If we don't deliver at least 3 valid exclusive leads in your territory within the first 30 days after your campaign goes live, your first month is refunded. Cancelling is just as simple — email us or message the chat, and your subscription ends at the close of the period you've already paid for. No phone calls, no hoops, no cancellation fee.

This Refund Policy is part of our Terms of Service and applies to subscriptions purchased from OYZLA LEADS LLC ("Oyzla Leads," "we," "us") at oyzlaleads.com.

1. Pricing and Automatic Renewal

What you pay: $1,000 for your first month, then $2,000/month — both before applicable tax. Your subscription is month to month with no long-term contract, and it renews automatically each month: your payment method on file is charged at the start of each billing period until you cancel.

There are no per-lead charges and no setup fees. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Any applicable sales or similar taxes are added at checkout as required by law.

2. The 3-Lead First-Month Guarantee

If we don't deliver at least 3 valid exclusive leads in your approved territory within the first 30 days after your campaign goes live, we refund your first month in full.

  • "Valid" lead means a prospect with working contact information, located in (or seeking coverage in) your approved territory, who expressed interest in an insurance product for a line your agency writes. A lead is not invalid merely because it doesn't result in a sale, doesn't respond to your outreach, or chooses another provider.
  • "Campaign goes live" means the date your advertising campaign begins running, which follows your completion of the short onboarding questionnaire after checkout. If onboarding information is incomplete, the 30-day window starts once the campaign actually launches.
  • The guarantee applies once per agency, to the first month of a new territory subscription.

3. How to Request a Refund

  • Email info@oyzlaleads.com (or simply reply to any email from us, or message the chat on our website) within 14 days after your 30-day guarantee window ends. One sentence is enough — no forms, no calls.
  • We confirm the lead count for your territory and respond by email.
  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days of approval.

4. Territory-Availability Refund

One agency per area is the whole model. If the territory you request at checkout is already taken, we'll offer you the nearest available adjacent territory. If you don't accept the alternative, your first payment is refunded in full — just tell us by email or chat.

5. Cancelling Your Subscription

  • How to cancel: email info@oyzlaleads.com (or reply to any of our emails, or message the chat) any time before your next renewal date. That's the entire process.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period — you keep receiving leads until then.
  • There is no cancellation fee and no long-term contract.
  • Other than the guarantee in Section 2, the territory refund in Section 4, or where required by law, fees already paid are not refunded or prorated for partial months.
  • When your subscription ends, your territory becomes available to other agencies.

6. Failed or Disputed Payments

If a renewal payment fails, we may pause lead delivery and campaign activity until payment is completed, and territory exclusivity may lapse after a reasonable period following notice. If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us first — we resolve billing questions quickly by email.
