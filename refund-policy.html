Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We want you to be happy with what we build. This policy explains how setup fees, monthly fees, and cancellations work.

Monthly service — cancel anytime

Your monthly hosting and maintenance fee is billed in advance and recurs until you cancel. You can cancel at any time; cancellation stops all future billing. To cancel, email oyzla.ai@gmail.com — we'll confirm and stop the next charge. Monthly fees already paid for the current period are generally non-refundable, since the service (hosting and maintenance) was provided during that period.

Setup fees

Setup fees cover the design and build of your custom tool. Before build begins, a setup fee is fully refundable. Once we've started building, setup fees are refundable on a pro-rated basis for work not yet performed, at our reasonable discretion. Once a build is delivered, setup fees are non-refundable.

If something's wrong

If a tool we built isn't working as agreed, tell us and we'll fix it — that's what the monthly maintenance is for. We'd always rather make it right than leave you unhappy.

What you keep

Because you own your deliverables, cancelling means future billing stops and ongoing hosting/maintenance ends, but the work created for you remains yours.

Contact

Questions, or want to cancel? Email oyzla.ai@gmail.com.
