What is approval-gated AI, and why does it matter for a small business?

Approval-gated AI does the repetitive work and then stops. It drafts the invoice, the reply or the quote, and nothing goes out under your name until you approve it. That is how every Oyzla AI build in Saratoga Springs, Utah works by default.

Short answer: approval-gated AI is an AI employee that drafts but cannot send. It prepares the invoice, customer reply, quote or schedule change, puts it in front of you, and only acts after you approve. You keep the final say; the AI does the typing.

Questions about approval-gated AI

What does approval-gated AI mean?

It means the AI can draft and prepare work, but it cannot send, book or publish anything under your name until you approve it. You review each item and approve it from one dashboard.

Why would a business want an AI that waits for approval?

Because the owner stays accountable for what goes to customers. A drafted reply or invoice that you read first cannot surprise you, and approval gating means you decide what leaves the building.

Does approval-gated AI slow my team down?

The slow part of most office work is the drafting, chasing and data entry, not the click. The AI does the drafting; you spend seconds approving instead of minutes writing.

What can the AI do without asking?

Drafting and preparing work for your review. Anything that goes out under your name, such as emails, quotes and invoices, waits for your approval.

Does Oyzla AI hold my passwords?

No. You connect your own accounts through OAuth, so we never hold your passwords.

Is approval-gated AI the same as a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions. An approval-gated AI employee takes on a repetitive job such as follow-ups, quoting, scheduling or document entry, and hands you a finished draft to approve.
