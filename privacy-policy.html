This Privacy Policy explains how Oyzla AI ("we," "us") handles information when you visit our website or engage our services.

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us and retain it only as long as needed to provide our services or as required by law. Because you connect your own accounts, you retain control over that access and can revoke it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete information we hold, subject to any legal obligations. Email oyzla.ai@gmail.com and we'll help.

Changes
