Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026 · Questions? Message us through the chat or email info@oyzlaleads.com.

1. Who We Are

Oyzla Leads ("Oyzla," "we," "us," or "our") operates oyzlaleads.com and provides exclusive local consumer-insurance leads to independent insurance agencies in the United States. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy covers three groups of people: (a) visitors to our website, (b) agency customers who subscribe to our service, and (c) consumers who respond to our advertising and ask to be connected with a local insurance agency.

2. Information We Collect

From website visitors:

  • Basic usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), browser type, device type, and referring page.
  • Anything you send us through the chat or by email.

From agency customers:

  • Business and contact details you provide at checkout and in our onboarding questionnaire — such as agency name, contact name, email address, territory preference, the insurance lines you sell, and the CRM you use.
  • Billing information, which is collected and processed by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

From consumers responding to our advertising:

  • Contact details you submit in our forms — typically name, email address, telephone number, and ZIP code — along with the type of insurance you're interested in and any details you choose to share about your coverage needs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the service: building and running local advertising campaigns, delivering consumer leads to the one subscribing agency for their territory, and supporting customers through chat and email.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions (via Stripe).
  • To respond to questions and requests sent through the chat or email.
  • To improve our website, forms, and campaigns.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.
For consumers: if you submit a request through one of our advertising forms, your information is shared with the single independent insurance agency that serves your area so they can follow up with a quote. We do not sell the same request to multiple agencies, and we do not sell your information to data brokers.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and advertising may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working properly, remember basic preferences, measure how visitors use the site, and measure the performance of our advertising campaigns (for example, conversion tracking from ad platforms). You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some site features. We do not use cookies to build profiles for sale to third parties.

5. Who We Share Information With

  • The subscribing agency for your territory — consumer lead details are delivered to the one agency that holds that territory, for insurance-quoting purposes only.
  • Stripe — payment processing for agency subscriptions. Stripe's own privacy policy governs the payment data it collects.
  • Service providers — companies that help us run the business, such as website hosting, form and data storage, email delivery, chat support, and advertising platforms. They may process information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Oyzla, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers — if Oyzla is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share consumer lead information with anyone other than the single agency described above and the service providers that make delivery possible.

6. Your Choices and Data Requests

  • Access, correction, deletion. You may ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Message us through the chat or email info@oyzlaleads.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, and within any timeline required by applicable state privacy law.
  • Consumers: if you submitted a quote request and no longer want to be contacted, tell the agency directly or contact us and we will pass the request along and remove your details from our active records.
  • Email marketing: any marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Depending on your state (for example, California, Colorado, Virginia, Utah, or Texas), you may have additional rights, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We honor applicable state privacy rights for all US residents.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here: customer account records for the life of the subscription plus the period needed for tax, accounting, and legal requirements; consumer lead records for as long as reasonably needed to deliver and support the service; and website analytics on a rolling basis. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website and reputable third-party providers for payments and data storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party services such as Stripe checkout. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted on this page and, for active customers, noted by email where appropriate.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, message us through the chat on our website or email info@oyzlaleads.com. Oyzla Leads is based in Saratoga Springs, Utah, USA.
