Questions we get asked

Can AI really help a commercial cleaning company?

Yes, for the repetitive office work: replying to inquiries, following up on quotes, scheduling messages, invoices and data entry. The AI drafts and you approve. We do not claim it replaces your crews or your judgment, and we make no promise about business results.

Will the AI send things to my customers without asking me?

No. Everything is approval-gated by default. Drafts come to you, and nothing goes out under your name until you approve it.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

No. You connect your accounts yourself through OAuth. We never ask for or hold your credentials.

How much does it cost?

Our typical build is an estimate of about $5,000 to set up plus about $2,000 a month to run, with the exact number confirmed on a short call. If you would rather prove it first, the paid pilot is $1,500 plus $300 a month for one workflow, live in about two weeks, with 100% of the pilot fee credited toward your full setup if you move forward within 30 days. Prices are estimates, not offers, plus applicable sales tax, and scope is confirmed in writing before any work begins.

How long until it is live?

A pilot workflow is live in about two weeks. Bigger builds take longer and we tell you up front.

Do you work with companies outside Utah?

We are based in Saratoga Springs, Utah, and Utah businesses are our focus. If you are elsewhere, contact us and we will tell you straight whether we can help.

Is this software I set up myself?

No. Oyzla AI is a managed service. We design, build, host and run the tools on our own accounts and hosting. The monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring and improvements, and the live system stops if the service ends.
