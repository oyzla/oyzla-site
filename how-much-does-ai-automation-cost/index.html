What the money pays for

  • Managed, not a handover: the live system depends on our hosting and management, so it runs for as long as the service does.

What moves the price

Mainly the number of workflows and how custom they are. One standard workflow, such as answering after-hours messages or drafting quotes for you to approve, is a smaller project than several connected workflows or a custom CRM.
