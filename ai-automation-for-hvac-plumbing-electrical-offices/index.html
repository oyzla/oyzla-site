Questions from trades offices

What can an HVAC, plumbing or electrical office hand to AI?

The repetitive office work: customer replies, after-hours and overflow messages, priced quotes, scheduling, invoices and the paperwork that follows a job. Oyzla AI builds AI employees that draft these for you to approve, then hosts and runs them.

Does the AI answer calls?

No. Our AI Front Desk covers web and email: an after-hours and overflow responder where urgent messages get flagged and nothing sits overnight. We do not describe it as a phone service.

Can it quote jobs?

The Scheduling and Quoting Engine builds priced quotes and booked time slots from your own rules, prices and calendar. You approve before anything goes to a customer.

Can it handle invoices and paperwork?

Yes. Document and Data Automation reads invoices, intake forms, orders and reports, extracts the details and files them where they belong.

Does it send anything without my approval?

No. Everything is approval-gated by default, and nothing goes out under your name until you approve it.

Do I have to set the software up myself?

No. Oyzla AI is a managed service: we design, build, host and run it, and you connect your own accounts through OAuth. We never hold your passwords.

How much does it cost?

Our typical build is an estimate of about $5,000 to set up plus about $2,000 a month to run, confirmed on a short call. The paid pilot is $1,500 plus $300 a month for one workflow, live in about two weeks. Prices are estimates, not offers, plus applicable sales tax, and scope is confirmed in writing before any work begins.
