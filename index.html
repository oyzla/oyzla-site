Our process

From "we should automate that"
to done, in order.

No jargon, no six-month roadmap. Four steps, and the last one never really ends — we keep it running for you.

01

Tell us what to automate

Describe the repetitive work in plain words — unanswered messages, re-typed data, manual quotes, scheduling ping-pong. If it eats your week, it's a candidate.

02

A short, jargon-free call to walk through your day. We pick what to automate first and agree exact scope and cost — before you commit a thing.

We design and build your custom tool, tuned to your business and your voice. The first version goes live in days — then improves week over week.

Everything we create is yours — code, content, accounts. We host, monitor, and keep tuning it as your business changes. No lock-in, ever.
