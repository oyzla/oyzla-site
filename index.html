FAQ
Do you need my passwords?

No. When an automation needs to touch one of your accounts, you connect it yourself through your own login (OAuth). We design around secure connections — never around your credentials.

Can the AI send things on its own?

Only what you approve. Everything is approval-gated by default — drafts come to you, and nothing goes out under your name until you say so.

Who owns what?

You own the design, the configuration and the IP. The live system runs on our keys and hosting as a managed service, and it ends with the contract. Your own data and accounts were always yours.

What if something breaks?

That's what the monthly covers. Your tools run on our servers and our accounts, so when something upstream changes or your business does, we fix it. You're not maintaining anything.

What does the monthly actually pay for?

Hosting, monitoring, maintenance and improvements. When an API changes, a workflow needs adjusting, or your business adds a new process — we handle it.

How long until it's live?

A pilot workflow is live in about two weeks. Bigger builds take longer, and we'll tell you up front. It improves week over week after launch.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Billing stops at the end of the current paid period, and the service stops with it. On request we export the data we hold for you. Our refund & cancellation policy spells out exactly what stops.
